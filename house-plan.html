Before building

Designs A and B assume a 30 × 60 ft footprint; Design C is drawn on 27 ft east-west × 53 ft north-south (1,431 sq ft). Each is assumed to be buildable footprint, with open space outside the walls and east-side access at the northeast end. No parking, stairs or setbacks are allocated within it.

This is a concept, not a sanctioned or construction drawing. A local architect / engineer must resolve wall thickness, clearances, structure, drainage, waterproofing, ventilation, shading, setbacks and local building rules. Not a Vastu-certified layout.
